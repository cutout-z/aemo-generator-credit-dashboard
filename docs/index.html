Curtailment Proxy

Methodology
Shortfall % = 1 − (SCADA / AVAILABILITY)
Compares actual output (SCADA) to the generator's bid-in availability (DISPATCHLOAD AVAILABILITY). A forecast-to-output shortfall proxy — not a measured curtailment.
Cannot separate grid constraints from outages: availability includes outages, and SCADA quality flags do not explain why output was limited. No causal split is published (metric v2.0-proxy).

Estimated Economic Curtailment

Methodology
Econ curtailment = shortfall below AVAILABILITY during RRP < $0 / total AVAILABILITY
Estimates energy not generated during negative spot price periods — a proxy for voluntary economic curtailment where generators bid off to avoid paying to export. It is the negative-price part of the curtailment proxy, so it never exceeds it.
Proxy only: cannot distinguish voluntary bid-off from AEMO dispatch instructions without bid data.

Price Capture

Methodology
Captured = Σ(MWh × RRP) / Σ(MWh)
Volume-weighted average price received vs. the simple average regional price over the intervals this unit reported SCADA. For a unit that reports every interval that is the regional time-weighted average; a unit with gaps (new, retiring or offline telemetry) is compared with its own intervals only. Ratio < 1.0 means the generator captures below-average prices (common for solar due to price cannibalisation).

Spot Price Exposure

Methodology
Bin share = Σ bin energy (MWh) / Σ generation (MWh) over all months with output
Distribution of generation volume across spot price buckets ($/MWh). Shows what proportion of energy was dispatched during negative, low, moderate, or high price periods. Shares are generation-weighted across months: zero-output months carry no weight, and the histogram is omitted when the whole window has no output.
High exposure to <$0 indicates revenue erosion risk without a PPA floor.

Energy offers — this unit

Methodology
This unit's energy offer behaviour from BIDDAYOFFER_D (daily 10-band offer prices, latest rebid version) and BIDPEROFFER_D ENERGY volumes (10-band availability). A day's stack is the day's mean offered MW per band; BANDAVAIL tranches are incremental, so the curve is cumulative offered MW as price rises.
Offers are intent, not dispatch outcomes or settled revenue (offer_based_estimate). Unit-level block: shown whenever this unit's offer data exists, independently of the regional FCAS block (thin or absent FCAS history must not hide it).

Regional Price Spreads (BESS Arbitrage)

Methodology
Daily top/bottom capture-window average prices: the simple mean of the 5-minute regional prices in each window (each interval weighted equally, not by volume) — the realisable discharge-value and charge-cost windows. A day runs 00:05–24:00 (interval-ending), so the interval ending at midnight belongs to the day it closes. The 1h/2h/4h/8h selector takes the top and bottom (duration × 12) five-minute intervals of each day, matching the cycling window of that battery duration; wider windows blend in mid-priced hours, so the realisable spread falls as duration grows. 'Decile' (fixed top/bottom 10% ≈ 2.4h) is the legacy 1–2h-battery proxy, kept for continuity with the AEMO QED benchmark. Market-level data: applies to every unit in the region.
Derived from AEMO DISPATCHPRICE 5-minute data. Quarterly context: AEMO QED reported NEM fleet charge/discharge spreads of $121/MWh in Q1 2026 and $51/MWh in Q2 2026.
